Service 03 // Enterprise Knowledge & Generative AI

Make Enterprise Knowledge Instantly Accessible

Transform fragmented documents, engineering knowledge and enterprise information into secure, intelligent systems that help teams find reliable answers and work more efficiently.

Business Problems

Operational Challenges This Service Solves

Knowledge Trapped in Documents

Engineering manuals, SOPs, contracts, and past ticket resolutions are scattered across drives and folders.

Unreliable Generic AI Answers

Standard chatbots lack internal context and fabricate plausible-sounding responses without source verification.

Permission & Privacy Concerns

Teams cannot index sensitive internal files without strict role-based filtering and data isolation.

What We Deliver

Concrete Engineering Deliverables

  • Layout-aware document ingestion handling PDFs, tables, and multi-column manuals
  • Hybrid retrieval combining vector embeddings, keyword search, and reranking
  • Citation-backed enterprise assistants enforcing document-level permissions
  • Automated LLM evaluation suites measuring groundedness and retrieval recall
Key Capabilities

Supporting Technical Disciplines

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • Custom LLM Applications
  • Hybrid Search & Vector Databases
  • Knowledge Graphs
  • LLM Evaluation & Grounding
  • Multilingual AI Assistants
Practical Use Cases

Where Teams Apply This Capability

Engineering Knowledge Assistants

Help field technicians and engineers query equipment manuals and fault histories with page citations.

Enterprise Document Intelligence

Compare contracts, summarize technical specifications, and extract structured clauses reliably.

Technical Troubleshooting Assistants

Surface verified past resolutions and runbooks directly inside service desk workflows.

Implementation Approach

Structured Delivery in Four Stages

01 / Discover

Audit document formats, update frequency, access-control rules, and common user queries.

02 / Design

Architect chunking strategies, hybrid vector + lexical indexing, and citation-first prompts.

03 / Build

Implement ingestion, reranking, and evaluation pipelines scored against golden question sets.

04 / Deploy & Improve

Launch with incremental document sync, user feedback capture, and faithfulness monitoring.
