What is included in the Pro Plan AI Assistant?
The Pro Plan (₹1,299/mo) includes an AI WhatsApp Assistant that answers customer order-status questions, shares receipts, and helps book trial dates, with ₹2,000/month in AI message credits included.
How does the Automated Instagram Weekly Backup work?

Yes. The interface is mobile responsive so master tailors can check measurements, view fabric photos, and update order stages from any smartphone or tablet.
