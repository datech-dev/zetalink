Company Overview

An Enterprise AI Transformation & Engineering Partner

Headquartered in Perambur, Tamil Nadu, India, Zetalink Technologies was established over two years ago with a clear engineering focus: helping businesses adopt practical, production-grade technology systems that solve measurable operational challenges.

We don't simply build software. We engineer intelligent systems—combining Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Agentic workflows, Machine Learning, MLOps, and our ZetaShield 360 data protection framework—so growing and established enterprises can adopt AI with architectural clarity and operational confidence.
