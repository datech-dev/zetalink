Business Challenges

Where Traditional Automation Breaks Down

Layout & Format Variability

Every vendor invoice, shipping notice, or customer email looks different—causing template-based OCR and brittle RPA scripts to fail repeatedly.

Manual Reconciliation Bottlenecks

Operations teams spend hours cross-checking PO numbers, tax fields, and email attachments across disconnected systems.

Silent Automation Errors

Without confidence scoring and validation rules, automated scripts propagate bad records downstream into accounting or inventory ledgers.

What Zetalink Delivers

End-to-End Intelligent Automation Capabilities

Workflows combining event triggers, AI classification, business-rule checks, and automated downstream execution.

AI & RPA Integration

Document Processing Pipelines

High-accuracy parsing of PDFs, scans, tables, and attachments into validated, ERP-ready data structures.
