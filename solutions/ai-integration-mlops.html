Business Challenges

Bridging the Gap Between Prototype Code and Production SLAs

Fragile Notebook-to-API Handoffs

Experimental models often lack proper dependency pinning, input validation, concurrency scaling, and graceful error handling.

Zero Visibility Into Model Behavior

Without trace logging and automated quality checks, teams cannot detect accuracy decay, prompt regressions, or runaway token spend.

Disconnected Enterprise Software

Standalone AI apps force users to switch tabs and copy-paste data, killing adoption across operational teams.
