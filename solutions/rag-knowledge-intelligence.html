Make Your Enterprise Knowledge Intelligent, Searchable, and Actionable.

Unlock institutional intelligence trapped across documents, databases, tickets, and shared drives with production Retrieval-Augmented Generation built for accuracy, permissions, and verifiable citations.

Business Challenges

Why Enterprise Knowledge Remains Hard to Access

Even with modern cloud storage, teams lose hours every week hunting across disconnected systems for authoritative answers.

Siloed Documents & Systems

Technical specifications, SOPs, contracts, and past resolutions live across shared drives, wikis, PDFs, and databases that keyword search barely bridges.

Naive RAG Hallucinations

Basic chunk-and-prompt prototypes pull irrelevant passages, lose table context, and fabricate confident-sounding answers without traceable citations.

Access Control Exposure

Indexing all company documents into a single flat vector store risks surfacing restricted HR, financial, or customer data to unauthorized roles.

Service Scope

What Zetalink Delivers

Complete retrieval and knowledge engineering designed for precision, source transparency, and continuous document synchronization.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

End-to-end grounded generation architectures connecting LLMs dynamically to verified internal documents and live structured stores.

Enterprise Knowledge Bases

Unified internal knowledge hubs with automated connectors, incremental document sync, and freshness tracking.

Hybrid Search & Vector Databases

Production vector indexing paired with BM25 lexical search so both semantic queries and exact identifiers retrieve cleanly.

Semantic Search & Reranking

Two-stage retrieval pipelines using domain embeddings and cross-encoder reranking to surface the highest-evidence passages.

Document Processing & Intelligence

Structure-preserving parsing for multi-column PDFs, spreadsheets, scanned files, and embedded tables.

Knowledge Graph Integration

Combining vector retrieval with entity-relationship graphs for multi-hop reasoning across connected assets, regulations, or parts.

Technical Architecture

Built for Retrieval Precision & Hallucination Reduction

  • Hierarchical Chunking & Embeddings: Parent-child document segmentation preserving section headings, tables, and surrounding context.
  • Hybrid Retrieval & Reranking: Reciprocal rank fusion across vector and keyword indexes followed by cross-encoder reranking.
  • Context Management: Token budget optimization, deduplication, and relevance thresholds that abstain when source evidence is missing.
  • Automated RAG Evaluation: Continuous measurement of retrieval recall, context precision, groundedness, and answer faithfulness.
