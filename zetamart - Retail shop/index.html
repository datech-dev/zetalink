Inside Zetamart Retail

Built for speed at the billing counter and clear operational control for store owners.

Responsive Billing & Cart Engine

Let cashiers scan items, look up customer credit or loyalty details, complete payments, and print receipts without relying on a mouse.

  • F1 key shortcut clears the current sale state cleanly for the next customer.
  • Loyalty Wallet lets customers redeem earned points directly at checkout.
  • Thermal Print templates formatted for standard 80mm and 58mm receipt printers.

FEFO & FIFO Batch Tracking

Reduce spoilage and maintain freshness across perishable or dated goods by automatically drawing stock from the oldest active batch.

  • Expiry Warnings alert store managers when batches approach sell-by dates.
  • Stock Audits let staff log physical counts and reconcile discrepancies.
  • Ledger Records maintain detailed histories for every inventory adjustment.

Role-Based Permissions Synchronization

Prevent unauthorized inventory edits or margin views. Navigation and export access align directly with assigned staff roles.

  • Menu Controls: Restricted modules such as Reports stay hidden from cashier logins.
  • Endpoint Guards: Exports, ledger logs, and analytics calls are enforced server-side.
  • Live Updates: Active sessions refresh permissions automatically when an admin updates roles.
